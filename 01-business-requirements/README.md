# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

Ann Ewah Business Operations is a simulated business operations environment created to demonstrate the design and implementation of a practical ClickUp workflow.

The project focuses on organizing client onboarding and administrative work into a structured, trackable workflow.

## Business Problem

The business needs a consistent way to manage incoming client requests, assign work to the appropriate staff members, track progress, handle missing information, and monitor work through completion.

## Initial Requirements

The workflow should allow the business to:

- Capture new client onboarding requests.
- Record and organize client information.
- Identify the type of client request.
- Track the current stage of each request.
- Assign work to the appropriate staff member.
- Track tasks that depend on other tasks.
- Handle situations where information is missing.
- Escalate requests when necessary.
- Automate predictable actions where appropriate.
- Provide visibility into outstanding work.

## Key Business Questions

Before implementing the system, the following questions should be considered:

- How are client requests currently received?
- Who receives and records them?
- What information is required before processing can begin?
- What information is commonly missing?
- Who determines task priority?
- Who determines task assignment?
- Which tasks require approval?
- Which tasks depend on other tasks?
- What situations require escalation?
- Which activities are repetitive enough to automate?
- What information does management need to monitor?

## Design Principle

The ClickUp system should be designed from the business requirements first rather than selecting ClickUp features simply because they are available.

Business requirement → Workflow decision → ClickUp implementation
